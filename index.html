The Sputtering Process / 3 Steps
Magnetron PVD - Physical Vapor Deposition
01 Process Optimization
Plasma Formation
High voltage ionises argon gas, creating Ar+ ions and free electrons confined by crossed ExB fields near the target surface.
ExB drift - Avalanche ionisation cascade
02 Target Erosion
Target Erosion
Accelerated Ar+ ions strike the copper target, transferring momentum to lattice atoms and ejecting them from the surface.
Y(E) - Yamamura/Bohdansky-style energy fit
03 Film Growth
Film Growth
Ejected atoms traverse the chamber and condense on the substrate, building a uniform thin film layer nanometer by nanometer.
Cosine distribution - Thickness uniformity
Model Note
Reduced-order browser model grounded in sputter yield, transport, and thin-film growth trends.
