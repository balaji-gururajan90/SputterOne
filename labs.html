By the end of these guided modules, students will develop a foundational understanding of how pressure, power, geometry, reactive sputtering, and co-sputtering influence thin-film deposition.

How to use this page

Recommended classroom use

Students should complete the modules in sequence, export CSV data after each run, and summarize the observed trend before progressing to the next experiment.

What students learn

  • How chamber pressure changes mean free path and scattering.
  • How power affects discharge state, ion energy, and sputtering yield.
  • How geometry, reactive gas, and co-sputtering alter film outcomes.

Guided Module Pathway

Each experiment below includes an objective, controlled variables, suggested starting conditions, recordable outputs, and reflection prompts. Complete the current module to unlock the next one.

Virtual Lab Modules are intended for qualitative trend learning, guided laboratory exercises, and structured classroom discussion. They do not replace calibrated industrial process development, but they do provide a strong framework for understanding cause-and-effect relationships in sputtering.
